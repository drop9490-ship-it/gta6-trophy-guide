      The manual-save plan

      GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game+, but it does have 15 manual save slots plus one autosave (PowerPyx, S1). Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter — 10 saves — and rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so do not use them on your platinum save.

      Not missable — do not worry

      Every story trophy is automatic and cannot be missed (PowerPyx, S1). Optional missions that disappear do not affect trophies, quest givers who vanish temporarily come back, and every trophy-related character is still available after the story in free-roam.

        About this atlas

Every guide cites its sources beside the trophy: a specific place or step with no source earns no trust, so where a method is still unconfirmed this atlas says so plainly instead of inventing one.
